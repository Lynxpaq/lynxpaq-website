One stock, two counters

Sell the last sofa once, not twice.

When a store sells on Instagram, on WhatsApp and in the shop, stock lives in three places and the same item gets sold twice. With Lynx E-commerce, the website and the shop counter draw from one stock count, so a sale in one place shows in the other straight away.
