The bar is part of the hotel

Every sale rings into the same ledger and draws down the same stock. Charge it to a room or take cash, and close the shift with a till reconciliation.

  • Charge to room by booking reference
  • Stock deducted at the moment of sale
  • Shift close with cash reconciliation

Stock that refuses to go negative quietly

A movement that would take stock below zero is refused and reported. An override is allowed, and logged against the person who made it.

  • Re-order levels and low-stock alerts
  • Buy by the crate, sell by the bottle
  • Stock value always current

The night audit that checks the costs too

Each night closes revenue and costs together. Every cost is compared with its own history at your hotel, and anything out of pattern is flagged.

  • Margin for the day, not just revenue
  • Runs automatically at the time you set
  • Once run, the day is frozen and stamped

A P&L you did not have to assemble

Where the money goes

A hotel rarely loses money all at once. It leaks.

Most hotel systems count what comes in. Lynx HMS also watches what goes out. One night on the demonstration property, the night audit flagged a single line: generator fuel.

3.88×generator fuel against its own 30-day average
₦534,336spent above the usual run-rate, in a single night
19.5%margin that night, against roughly 32% on a normal one

Cash sales that are never rung upBar sales draw stock down as they are made. Stock that moves without a sale behind it is raised as a discrepancy, not discovered at stocktake.
Rooms let for cash and shown as vacantRoom status, the folio and the night audit are the same record. A room cannot be earning and empty at once.
Costs inflated or inventedEvery cost is compared with its own history and flagged when it breaks pattern. The audit also counts expenses entered without a receipt number.
Yesterday quietly rewrittenA completed audit cannot be edited. Every change in the system goes to an audit log with the user, the time and the detail.
Offline operation

When the power or network goes, the front desk keeps working.

Walk-ins, check-ins, check-outs, cash and bar sales are queued on the device and sent up when the connection returns. Each carries its own reference, so a replay cannot double-charge a guest or double-count a sale.

Anything that cannot be reconciled cleanly goes to a needs-attention list for a person to settle. Nothing is guessed in the background.

Smart solutions. Better hospitality.

Lynxpaq Technologies is an Abuja software company that builds tools for Nigerian hospitality businesses. Lynx HMS Web is our flagship product.
